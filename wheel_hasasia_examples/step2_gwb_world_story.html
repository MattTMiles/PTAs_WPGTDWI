The argument, step by step — Step 2: the background returns¶

Second notebook of the series. Step 1 built the clean world and measured the folklore being true in it. This step flips one toggle — gwb — and the NG15-amplitude gravitational-wave background returns, doing its two jobs at once:

  1. Detection floor. $A_{\rm gwb}=6.4\times10^{-15}$ enters the hasasia curves as self-noise. Step 1 §1b already previewed the number: 5110 detected sources at 25 yr collapse to 5.
  2. Comb jitter. The sea's per-bin strain rides on every comb's phase: $\sigma_{\rm sea}=h_{\rm sea,bin}(f)/h$ in quadrature with the white term, where $h_{\rm sea,bin}(f)=A(f/f_{\rm yr})^{-2/3}/\sqrt{fT}$ — a stated DEMO-glue convention (the campaign's calibrated version is its confusion machinery; this is its shape).

Everything else — population (V1, 18,727 sources), array, fixed pulsar, certification bar — is byte-identical to step 1. The measurement below separates the two jobs, because they are usually conflated and they die at different links of the chain.

5 — Closing frame¶

The chain of step 1 breaks at its first link:

$$\underline{\text{detection}}\;\to\;\text{attribution}\;\to\;\text{combs}\;\to\; \text{evidence}\;\to\;\text{bar}.$$

The sea is not one more noise term — it is a floor that scales with the thing being searched for, which is why no span, and no wheel, out-waits it. What remains detectable are the loudest few monsters; whether they can carry certification is exactly where the campaign's loudness machinery (step 4) picks up.
