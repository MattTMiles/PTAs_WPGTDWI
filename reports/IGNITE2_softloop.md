### 1.2 The anatomy — why (a) and (c) are perfect while (b) is destroyed

The statistic has teeth exactly as designed: every wrong cert demands a source displacement
4–6 orders of magnitude beyond its σ (R_all 3×10⁴–8×10⁵ against a 9.2 bar; the |Δk| =
25–395 fringes are unhideable, and the R_det control landing at exactly its pre-recorded
87 % ceiling shows the co-registration idea is what does the work). The failure is on the
other side: **above onset the array-wide fringe field is itself poisoned.** The
leave-one-out reference solution u_R is dragged by the same confident wrong fringes
everywhere in the array (noise-locked MAP fringes with high q_max get large weights), so
u_R is significantly displaced from zero in essentially every realisation — and a TRUE
cert (u_a = 0) then fails concordance *with its own poisoned reference*. The veto measures
"this realisation's fringe field is discordant", which is true above onset for every
realisation, rather than "this pulsar disagrees with the others", which is what
certification purity needs. A per-pulsar veto built on a reference this contaminated
cannot be selective, at any threshold — the failure is structural, not a tuning artifact.

Note the asymmetry between the cells: at the VLBI onset cell (tight priors, ΣK 470) the
reference is cleaner and 67 % of true certs survive; at the lit onset cell (wide priors,
the wrong-cert factory) only 3 % do. The layer fails hardest exactly where it was needed
most.

### 1.3 What survives for the criterion

(c) = 100 % is the tantalising number: the co-registration statistic **does** buy back the
wrong-counterpart robustness D1 gave up — at the *realisation* level. A realisation-level
discordance flag (not a per-pulsar veto) is the natural salvage, but that is a new
criterion design, cronus's to make, not a fenced agent's. Recorded, not adopted, not run.

## 3. STAGE 2 — THE SOFT LOOP. VERDICT: **STOP (fourth mode) — but the anatomy inverts the hard-lock story.**

15 realisations per onset cell + 5 scrambled-source realisations per cell **through the
soft loop** (40 total; seeds 6.0xx / 6.5xx M). The loop, exactly as briefed: M-step fits
the source against **lnL_marg** (`trackB_b1.B1Marg` — spec §3's
`Q = Σ_p Σ_n q_p(n)·lnL`; `∇lnL_marg` IS the q-weighted Q-gradient; every one of the 116
pulsars fringe-marginalised at every step; **no term ever hard-locked**, mask ≡ 1) →
E-step re-score → detection + certification under criterion-v2 with the cell's fresh
Gumbel fN floor. No purity veto (Stage 0: not adopted). M-step mechanics: damped Newton
(damping 0.5) with FD gradient, iteration-0 FD Hessian (eigenvalue-floored), and a batched
3-candidate backtracking line search that accepts a step ONLY if lnL_marg + prior improves
— so a rejected step is a measured "the objective says stay", not a stall. σ(log10 mc) =
profile half-width of the marginalised objective along each mc dim (softcascade recipe).
Trajectories: `IGNITE2_results/ig2_soft_trajectories.png`; every per-iteration raw column
(`iter_dlnL/iter_qmax/iter_mapk`, cert identities with Δk) is banked per realisation.

### 3.2 The anatomy — every STOP is a SEED failure; the LOOP ITSELF is benign

**The wrong-certification cascade is GONE.** Across all 40 realisations and every
iteration: the wrong-cert count **never grows** (it is flat in every trajectory — compare
the hard lock's 3 → 116, 356/359 wrong); the false-fringe mass W is flat to ±1 (hard
lock: W = 115–116 from the first iteration); `src_mc_off` stays < 1e-4 dex in every
signal realisation (hard lock: up to 1.6 dex in one step). **The soft M-step at the true
source has ≈ zero gradient and the line search refuses to walk off the peak** — 24 of 30
signal realisations terminate in 2 iterations with every proposed step rejected.
**Tight local width + CORRECT global registration** this time: σ(log10 mc) of the
116-pulsar marginalised objective is 1e-5–1e-4 dex — far below ATLAS's 0.003-dex bound —
and it comes with dk = 0, not despite it.

**Where the loop moves, it moves toward the truth, both ways:**

- **Growth (2/30, both genuine, both dk = 0):** at the lit cell, J1327-0755 crosses the
  38.9-nat floor at iteration 2 of a smooth +27-nat lnL_marg climb and stays; at the VLBI
  cell, J1713+0747 rises 10.7 → 12.9 nat over 9 accepted steps (`lnmarg` +35 nat).
  The soft fit *polishes the source against the fringe mixture and lifts genuine
  fringe-breaking evidence over the floor.* This is the leak B1.3's hard lock could never
  express — real, but +1 cert per realisation, in 2 of 30, with no compounding.
- **Self-cleaning (3/6 detecting scrambled realisations):** the M-step takes one large step
  (0.2–0.35 scaled, +2 000–3 000 nat of lnL_marg — the scrambled source is nowhere near an
  optimum of loud real data), and the re-scored E-step **drops the false certification**
  (2→0, 1→0, 1→0). The hard lock did the opposite: its first step *created* 116 detections.
- **The one lost true cert is a margin event, not a wrong fix:** the VLBI realisation that
  shrank held J1909-3744 at `dlnL = 8.97` against the 7.59 floor — a 1.4-nat margin. Two
  polish steps later the gap statistic sits below the floor; **dk = 0 throughout**, the
  pulsar never leaves the true fringe. A floor-edge cert lost to a 1-nat wobble of a gap
  statistic — the D2 lesson (margins inside calibration scatter carry no weight) applied
  to a single object.

**Every surviving failure is upstream of the loop.** The 4 lit wrong-cert realisations
certify J1545-4550 (Δk = −115, twice), J1525-5545 (−129), J1045-4509 (+133), J1125-6014
(−114) **at iteration 0** — stage-1 impurity (§10.8.3's noise-lock class) inherited as
seeds, and the loop neither amplifies nor removes them (their dlnL never changes because
those realisations never accept a step). The 3 scrambled keepers are **small-|Δk|
noise-locks** — J0437-4715 at Δk = −4 (`dlnL = 11.83`, `qmax = 0.906`), B1937+21 at
Δk = +21, J0711-6830 at +231 — exactly D1's stated wrong-counterpart hole (`fN` has no
defence), exactly the events the purity layer was meant to kill and, per §1, cannot
(the J0437 keeper is the same small-|Δk| class as the (d) survivor; note it also clears
the **fALL** floor by 0.15 nat, inside that floor's own ±0.52 fit error — even the
blind-robust column leaks this one event).

### 3.3 What this settles

**B1.3's DAMPED and IGNITE's CASCADE were verdicts on the hard lock, and the spec's
prediction is now confirmed dynamically: replace the delta-function fringe commitment with
the fringe-posterior-weighted (marginalised) source update and the instability
disappears.** The soft loop does not compound (no cascade in 40/40), does not destroy
correct registration (0 wrong-fix losses; 1 floor-margin loss), occasionally lifts a
genuine borderline cert over the floor, and half the time cleans a wrong-counterpart
false alarm out on its own. Hogg's iterated phase-up, implemented as the spec wrote it,
is **dynamically safe and mildly consolidating — and it cannot rescue the criterion it
is fed by**: the seeds above onset are impure (§10.8.3), the purity layer failed its
pre-registration (§1), and the wrong-counterpart hole passes straight through the loop
when the noise-lock lands within a few fringes of truth. The certification bottleneck is
now entirely in the **criterion**, not in the **loop dynamics**.

### 4.1 Caveats that travel

- **15 realisations per cell** (5 skies × 3 weathers): GEO says the sky draw dominates
  yield variance, so per-cell rates carry ±0.2-class sky-sampling error; the *dynamical*
  statements (no cascade, no wrong-fix, flat W) are per-trajectory and do not share it.
- **The signal loops start at the true source** (the conditional convention, as B1.3 and
  IGNITE's hard loop did). "Stable at truth where the hard lock cascaded from truth" is
  the designed comparison; behaviour from a *mis-registered but unscrambled* start (tol
  grid through the soft loop) is untested here.
- σ(log10 mc) is the profile width of the fringe-marginalised objective + weak box prior —
  a conditional width of the M-step's own surface, not a posterior credible width.
- The M-step reuses the iteration-0 Hessian with a 3-candidate backtracking line search;
  steps are accepted only when lnL_marg + prior improves. A "no-step" iteration is a
  measured property of the objective at that point, not a solver failure — but a richer
  optimiser could in principle move where this one stayed (it would have to fight an
  lnL_marg *decrease* to do so).
- Fresh D2-sized floors exist at the two onset cells only; the other 22 cells' v2 floors
  in §4 are 10-null fits with their (large) errors stated. The fALL loop column uses the
  onset cells' 270-null fits.
- The scrambled-loop STOP statistics (6/10 detect) are consistent with, and add to, the
  banked hard-loop scrambled rate (2/5) — under a *higher* floor here (7.59 vs 5.46),
  because the truth draws and noise weathers differ per seed. Both are the fN family's
  wrong-counterpart hole, measured; neither is loop-generated.
